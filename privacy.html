Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who we are

"The Money Behind" is a YouTube channel. "Money Behind Studio" is the channel's internal publishing application, used only by the channel's own team to manage this channel.

What the application accesses

Money Behind Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to this channel, edit their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and playlists, manage comment moderation settings on this channel's videos, and read this channel's own analytics (such as views, watch time and estimated revenue). It is authorised only by the channel owner's Google account. It does not provide a login for anyone else, and it does not access any other person's Google account or YouTube data.

Google user data

The only Google user data processed is the channel owner's own channel data obtained through the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API. This data is used solely to publish and manage this channel. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or combined with data from other sources. Access tokens are stored encrypted on a private server and are never shared.

Retention and deletion

Analytics data for this channel is kept for up to 24 months. You can revoke the application's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; revoking access deletes stored tokens, and stored analytics are deleted on request by the channel owner.

Website visitors

This website sets no cookies and collects no personal data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Money Behind Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the channel's YouTube "About" page.
